Meet Our Team

Our coaching staff brings years of professional, collegiate, and high school baseball experience. Each coach is committed to player development and helping athletes reach their full potential on and off the field.

Willie James

Director of Operations / Owner

8-year professional playing career with the Atlanta Braves organization. Attended Arizona Western Community College and Purdue University. Played for Rome Braves, Myrtle Beach Pelicans, and Mississippi Braves. Retired in 2009 and has coached in the Myrtle Beach area for 13+ years.

Allen Cox

Director of College Placement / Hitting Coach

25 years of coaching experience. Regarded as one of the best hitting coaches in North America. Former hitting coach at Georgia Gwinnett College and head academy coach at Okotoks Dawgs in Canada. Scouted for the San Diego Padres and Milwaukee Brewers.

Willie-Willie James

Director of Player Development

Multi-sport athlete at Crenshaw High School. Played baseball and ran track at Los Angeles City College. Extensive coaching career spanning baseball, basketball, and football. California Inter Scholastic Coach of the Year (2003-2004). Founder of Love the Game Academy.

Enrique De Los Rios

Director of Facility Operations / Pitching Coach

Professional pitcher from San Antonio de los Altos, Venezuela. Signed by the Chicago Cubs at age 17. Pitched in Cubs organization (2013-2019) and winter ball for Leones de Caracas. Represented Venezuela in World Baseball Championships (2012).

Michael Frazier

Director of Athletic Performance

Certified strength and conditioning specialist focused on baseball-specific athletic performance, injury prevention, and player longevity. Develops customized training programs for all age groups.

Willie James

Director of Operations / Owner

Professional Playing Career: Willie James enjoyed an 8-year professional baseball career, primarily with the Atlanta Braves organization. He played for the Rome Braves, Myrtle Beach Pelicans, and Mississippi Braves, among other teams. He also competed in independent leagues throughout his career.

College Baseball: Willie attended Arizona Western Community College and Purdue University, where he honed his skills and developed his love for the game.

Coaching Philosophy: After retiring from professional play in 2009, Willie has dedicated over 13 years to coaching in the Myrtle Beach area. His vision for the Carolina Rays Organization is to develop players and allow them to create their own opportunities in baseball, enabling each athlete to pursue their dreams at whatever level they aspire to reach.

Commitment to Development: Willie emphasizes that success comes from hard work, dedication, and creating opportunities. He is passionate about helping young athletes build the skills, confidence, and mindset necessary to compete at the highest levels.

Allen Cox

Director of College Placement / Hitting Coach

Coaching Experience: Allen Cox brings 25 years of coaching expertise and is widely regarded as one of the best hitting coaches in North America.

College & Academy Roles:

  • Former Hitting Coach at Georgia Gwinnett College
  • Head Academy Coach at Okotoks Dawgs (Canada)
  • Coaching positions at Kentucky Wesleyan, Owensboro Catholic HS, Las Plumas HS
  • Coached with Regina Red Sox, Owensboro Oilers, Coastal Breeze, and various academy teams

Scouting Experience: Allen has worked as a professional scout for both the San Diego Padres and Milwaukee Brewers, giving him invaluable insight into what it takes to succeed at the next level.

Showcases & Camps: Allen has run and coached at numerous prestigious showcases and camps including:

  • All American Talent Showcase
  • Perfect Game events
  • Tournament 12
  • And many more regional and national showcases

Expertise: Known for his ability to break down swing mechanics, develop hitting approaches, and maximize offensive performance for players at all levels.

Willie-Willie James

Director of Player Development

Athletic Background: Willie-Willie was a multi-sport athlete at Crenshaw High School in Los Angeles, where he excelled in baseball and track & field. He continued his baseball and track career at Los Angeles City College.

Extensive Coaching Career: Willie-Willie has coached baseball, basketball, and football at various levels:

  • Vista Del Lago High School (California)
  • Valley View High School (California)
  • Texas Christian High School

International Experience: Served as Director of Player & Skills Development for the Italian Baseball League in Ravenna, Italy, bringing his expertise to players around the world.

Professional Roles:

  • Professional umpire in the California League
  • Scout for the Chicago White Sox
  • Scout for the Texas Rangers

Awards & Recognition:

  • California Inter Scholastic Coach of the Year (2003-2004)
  • Founder of Love the Game Academy
  • Numerous coaching accolades throughout his career

Personal: Willie-Willie is married with 4 children and 7 grandchildren. His wealth of experience and multi-generational perspective enriches his approach to player development.

Enrique De Los Rios

Director of Facility Operations / Pitching Coach

Professional Career: Enrique is a professional pitcher from San Antonio de los Altos, Venezuela. He was signed by the Chicago Cubs organization at just 17 years old and pitched in their minor league system from 2013-2019.

Winter Ball Experience: Competed in Venezuelan Winter League baseball for Leones de Caracas, one of the most prestigious teams in Latin American baseball.

International Competition: Represented Venezuela in the World Baseball Championships in 2012, showcasing his talents on the international stage.

Recognition: Received the Top Athlete Award in his hometown of San Antonio de los Altos in 2018.

Coaching Experience:

  • Guest instructor for Mt Vernon Patriots (12U team)
  • Assistant Coach at Myrtle Beach High School
  • Pitching Coach for Myrtle Beach High School JV team

Coaching Philosophy: Enrique is passionate about sharing his professional knowledge and experience with the Carolina Rays players. He specializes in pitching mechanics, velocity development, arm care, and mental preparation for pitchers at all levels.
