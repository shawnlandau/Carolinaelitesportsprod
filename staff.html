Willie James

Director of Operations / Owner

Professional Playing Career

Willie James brings an impressive 8-year professional playing career to Carolina Elite Sports. His journey through professional baseball includes:

  • Atlanta Braves Organization: Played for multiple affiliates including the Rome Braves, Myrtle Beach Pelicans, and Mississippi Braves
  • Independent Leagues: Continued professional career in independent baseball
  • College Baseball: Attended Arizona Western Community College and Purdue University
Coaching Experience

After retiring from professional baseball in 2009, Willie has dedicated the past 13+ years to coaching and developing players in the Myrtle Beach area. His coaching philosophy centers on helping players create their own opportunities in baseball.

Philosophy

"Carolina Rays Organization is designed to develop and allow its players to create their own opportunities in the game of baseball and to allow each player an opportunity to pursue a dream of playing baseball at whatever level the player dreams of playing."

Willie's firsthand experience navigating the professional baseball system, combined with his understanding of player development, makes him uniquely qualified to guide young athletes through their baseball journey.

Enrique De Los Rios

Director of Facility Operations / Pitching Coach

Professional Background

Hometown: San Antonio de los Altos, Venezuela

Professional Playing Career
  • Chicago Cubs Organization (2013-2019): Signed at age 17, pitched for 7 seasons in the Cubs minor league system
  • Venezuelan Winter League: Played for Leones de Caracas
  • International Competition: Represented Venezuela in the World Baseball Championships (2012)
Honors & Recognition
  • Top Athlete Award: Recognized in his hometown of San Antonio de los Altos (2018)
Coaching Experience

Since transitioning from professional play to coaching, Enrique has dedicated himself to developing the next generation of pitchers:

  • Mt Vernon Patriots (12U): Guest instructor
  • Myrtle Beach High School: Assistant coach
  • Myrtle Beach High School JV: Current pitching coach
  • Carolina Elite Sports: Pitching coach and Director of Facility Operations
Coaching Philosophy

Enrique brings the knowledge and experience gained from 7 years of professional baseball to every coaching session. His passion is sharing the techniques, mental approach, and work ethic that allowed him to succeed at the professional level with Carolina Rays players.

Having navigated the path from international prospect to professional pitcher, Enrique understands the dedication and development required at every level. He uses this firsthand experience to guide young pitchers toward their own dreams of playing at the highest levels.
