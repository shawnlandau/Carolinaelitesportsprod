Where Our Alumni Play

Professional Baseball

Several of our alumni have advanced to professional baseball, including MLB organizations and independent leagues. Their success is a testament to the quality of development and training they received at Carolina Elite Sports.

NCAA Division I

Many of our players have earned scholarships and opportunities to play at Division I programs across the country. From ACC to SEC conferences, our alumni compete at the highest level of college baseball.

NCAA Division II & III

We've helped numerous players find the right fit at Division II and Division III programs, where they continue their academic and athletic careers.

Junior College

Our alumni have found success at JUCO programs, using these opportunities as stepping stones to four-year universities and continued baseball careers.
