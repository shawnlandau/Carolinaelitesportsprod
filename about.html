About Carolina Elite Sports

Building Champions On and Off the Field

Our Mission

"Carolina Rays Organization is designed to develop and allow its players to create their own opportunities in the game of baseball and to allow each player an opportunity to pursue a dream of playing baseball at whatever level the player dreams of playing."

Carolina Elite Sports is dedicated to comprehensive player development at all levels. From young athletes ages 6 just starting their baseball journey to post-graduate players preparing for college and professional opportunities, we provide elite training and development programs tailored to each individual's needs and goals.

Our Approach

We believe in developing the complete player - focusing not just on physical skills, but also on building confidence, mental toughness, and a winning mindset. Our experienced coaches and full-time staff of instructors are available for private lessons and work individually with each athlete to help them reach their full potential.

Our coaching philosophy emphasizes creating opportunities for players at every level, whether that's making their high school team, earning a college scholarship, or pursuing professional baseball. We don't just train players - we prepare them for success on and off the field.

Our Facilities

We operate two state-of-the-art training facilities in the Myrtle Beach area, providing comprehensive training environments:

  • Indoor Training Facility: Located at 4770 Northgate Blvd, Myrtle Beach, SC 29577 - featuring climate-controlled training areas, batting cages, pitching lanes, and strength & conditioning equipment
  • Ripken Experience Facility: Located at 3051 Ripken Way, Myrtle Beach, SC 29577 - offering full-field training, game experience, and outdoor development opportunities
  • Main Office: 4007 Belle Terre Blvd Unit H, Myrtle Beach, SC 29579
