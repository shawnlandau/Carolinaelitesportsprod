About Carolina Elite Sports

A Tradition of Development

Our Mission

"Carolina Rays Organization is designed to develop and allow its players to create their own opportunities in the game of baseball and to allow each player an opportunity to pursue a dream of playing baseball at whatever level the player dreams of playing."

Carolina Elite Sports is dedicated to providing exceptional baseball training and player development for athletes of all ages. From youth players just starting their journey to post-graduate athletes preparing for the next level, we are committed to building skills, confidence, and character.

Who We Are

Located in North Myrtle Beach, South Carolina, Carolina Elite Sports is home to the Carolina Rays baseball program. We pride ourselves on being more than just a training facility – we're a community dedicated to excellence both on and off the field.

Our program features comprehensive training, state-of-the-art facilities, and individualized programs tailored to each athlete's needs and goals. We have a full-time staff of professional instructors available for private lessons, group training, and specialized clinics.

Our Approach

Our comprehensive training programs focus on:

  • Skill Development: Fundamentals, technique refinement, and advanced training methods
  • Mental Performance: Building confidence, focus, and competitive mindset
  • Physical Conditioning: Strength, speed, and agility training
  • Game Strategy: Baseball IQ and situational awareness
  • College Recruitment: Guidance and exposure for players seeking to play at the next level

Our Facilities

We operate two premier training facilities in the Myrtle Beach area:

Indoor Training Facility

3265 Waccamaw Boulevard, Myrtle Beach, SC 29579

Our state-of-the-art indoor facility features professional-grade batting cages, pitching areas, and training equipment to help players develop year-round.

Ripken Experience Facility

3051 Ripken Way, Myrtle Beach, SC 29577

Partner facility at the world-renowned Ripken Experience complex, providing access to premium fields for tournament play and outdoor training.
