About Carolina Elite Sports

Building Champions On and Off the Field

Our Mission

Carolina Elite Sports is dedicated to player development at all levels. From young athletes just starting their baseball journey to post-graduate players preparing for the next level, we provide comprehensive training and development programs.

Our Approach

We believe in developing the complete player - focusing not just on physical skills, but also on building confidence, mental toughness, and a winning mindset. Our experienced coaches work individually with each athlete to help them reach their full potential.

Our Facilities

We operate two state-of-the-art training facilities in Myrtle Beach:

  • Indoor Facility: Located at 4770 Northgate Blvd, featuring climate-controlled training areas
  • Ripken Experience Facility: Located at 3051 Ripken Way, offering field training and game experience

Why Choose Carolina Elite Sports?

  • Experienced coaching staff with proven track records
  • Comprehensive training programs for all skill levels
  • State-of-the-art facilities and equipment
  • Focus on both physical and mental development
  • Track record of player advancement to higher levels
