Post-Graduate Programs

Elite Player Development

Our post-graduate program is designed for high school graduates preparing for college baseball or the professional ranks. This intensive program focuses on:

  • Advanced Training: Professional-level skill development and refinement
  • Strength & Conditioning: Customized programs to maximize performance
  • Showcase Opportunities: Exposure to college coaches and scouts
  • Mental Performance: Sports psychology and competitive mindset training
  • Academic Support: Maintaining eligibility and academic progress

Program Benefits

  • Daily professional training
  • Access to both training facilities
  • Nutritional guidance
  • Video analysis and technology
  • College placement assistance
  • Tournament and showcase participation
